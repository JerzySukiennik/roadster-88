# Roadster 88: Space mode contract (web only)

Jurek's request (2026-10-17): a button on the tablet teleports you into space, armed like time travel (press once to arm, again to go). You arrive in low Earth orbit at the same date and time. In orbit you can change the year (before the Earth formed, the far future with megastructures you can fly up to) and see what the Earth looked like then. No gravity: the car floats, and to stop you have to turn around. The same button brings you back through a re-entry. Co-op: friends in orbit see each other. Round 3 (MAP-CONTRACT 4e): after re-entry you can land anywhere on the real Earth (D's patch generator).

## 2. How it works
- **Frame.** The car stays near `POCKET = (0, 6000, 0)` in the normal Rapier world, 6 km above the valley where no collider reaches. The pocket rides a real circular orbit (`orbit.js`): 400 km up, 51.6° inclination (ISS), 7.67 km/s, propagated in an inertial frame from the shared server clock (`Date.now() + online.offset`), so every client agrees where an orbit is. The pocket axes are the local orbital frame (`+y` radial up, `+z` north-ish along the ground, `+x` west), so the car keeps its heading at arrival and the ground scrolls underneath at the true speed (about 38 km in 5 s, one lap in 92 min). The orbital drive and recentring (every 2.6 km) move the orbit itself (`orbit.displace`), so physics never leaves the pocket. Near megastructures co-orbit (wheel station); Earth-fixed ones (tether, ring) are seen sweeping past or reached with the drive.
- **Departure** reuses the time machine: `space.launch()` sets `tc` to `departing` with the present as destination (saved destination, `last` and `jumps` are restored after), so `main.handleTimeEvents` plays the full departure (trails or ring, flash, booms, online flags). At `gone` `space.preStep` moves the body into the pocket, sets `sim.env.space = true` and switches the view; main's `arrive` restores velocity and plays the arrival vapour. `arriveValley()` runs harmlessly (same era early-out, `safeSpawnAt` returns the pocket point).
- **What is drawn.** The main camera switches to layer 31 only; the space root, the cars in space, fx, time fx, damage shards, remote cars, the driver and the sun, twilight, flash and lamp lights get layer 31 (only objects already on layer 0, so the layer-1 shadow proxies stay hidden). Nobody else's `visible` flags are touched. `valley.update`, `eras.update` and `life.update` are skipped while in space (shim, see R3).
- **Sky.** `sky.external = space` makes `SkySystem.setTime` return early (the one-line edit in `render/sky.js`); space then drives `sky.sunDir`, `sky.lightDir`, sun colour and intensity (eclipse and limb reddening), `sky.twilight` as earthshine from below, `sky.flashLight` as re-entry heat glow, `sky.exposure` (eye adaptation in Earth's shadow), fog off. `sky.update` still places the shadow box and captures the environment cube, which now sees an Earth backdrop proxy (`sky.addEnvProxy`), so the paint reflects the planet.
- **Backdrop** (`planet.js`): one camera-centred shell ray-traces the Earth per pixel at true scale in Earth-fixed kilometres: Blue Marble NG day map by season (8k on High, 4k otherwise, coast alpha from D's `assets/earth/mask_8k.png`), Black Marble lights by decade, clouds with parallax and shadows, GGX sun glint on water, GEBCO relief, single-scattering Rayleigh, Mie and an era haze layer through a Chapman-function sun path, airglow, the Moon (LROC colour and relief, Lommel-Seeliger, phase and position from the date, distance from deep-time tidal recession), the Sun disc (limb darkening, size and colour by age), the Milky Way (NASA SVS) and stars (Yale Bright Star Catalog, `stars.js`, hidden behind the Earth and Moon). Far structures are meshes in Earth kilometres inside a camera-scaled group (`structures.js`), hidden per pixel behind the Earth; near ones are true scale in the pocket.
- **Realism pass** (tuned side by side against ISS photos, `Niepotrzebne/space/porownanie_iss.jpg`): auto exposure meters the sunlit Earth (25 rays against the globe), so stars and the Milky Way fade out whenever the lit Earth is in frame and come back on the night side; the Sun is a tiny blinding disc with a tight corona and a weak lens flare (`gfx.updateSun` flare scaled by 0.3 in space, R9). The night side is black except city lights (point-like specks, warm sodium tint by decade), the faint green airglow line at 95 km and earthshine; the car gets hard sunlight plus blue-white earthshine only. Atmosphere: Rayleigh, Mie (0.012) and ozone through a ray march with samples packed around the closest approach, plus a cheap multiple-scattering term, so the limb is a thin white-blue to deep-blue band with orange and red bands at the terminator and aerial perspective toward the horizon. Surface: desaturated albedo (0.85), broad GGX glint on water only, soft clouds with parallax and shadows, relief at grazing light, soft terminator. Nadir detail (`detail.js`): within 3,200 km of the surface in the present, 4 x 4 NASA GIBS tiles (Blue Marble NG and VIIRS Black Marble, 500 m per pixel, 2 x 2 on Low) are streamed ahead of the ground track and blended in, so the ground under you is ten times sharper than the global map. GIBS is fetched live from `gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov` (CORS open, no key); offline or failing it falls back silently to the global map, `?nodetail` turns it off. Tone mapping stays ACES (AgX was tried and looked flatter against the photos).
- **Years** (`look.js`, keyed by E's `eraOf` ids plus the year): pre-solar nebula (< 4.568 Ga), protoplanetary disk with planetesimals (< 4.54 Ga), magma Earth, giant-impact debris disk, Hadean crust with lava and a huge close Moon, Archean orange haze, Proterozoic, Snowball Earth, PALEOMAP continents (Scotese 2018, CC BY 4.0) from 500 Ma to 5 Ma, rotated so a supercontinent is under you, Ice Age sheets and low seas, city lights 1880 to 2499 (none before electricity), the 2150 tether under construction, 2300 tether and wheel station, ruins and rewilding, 12,000 AD orbital ring, tether, station, patterned lights and a Dyson swarm, a broken ring in the next ice age, a debris ring in the distant future, Pangaea Ultima, a dying Earth with dry ocean basins and a bigger Sun, then (keypad years) a hot Earth, a runaway greenhouse, a lava world under the red giant and an engulfed Earth with a white dwarf.
- **Physics** (`sim/car.js` `spaceStep`, `sim/flight.js` `spaceUpdate`, only when `sim.env.space`): gravity scale 0, no tyres, suspension, aero or powertrain, wheels free-spin down. Thrust from the real 10-nozzle model through the allocator: W main engine (55 %), Shift full, Space lift (side pods), arrows pitch and roll, A/D yaw. Assist on: rate damping only (plus a small fine-attitude torque below the valve deadband). There are no brakes: S turns the nose retrograde (yaw first), and W while holding S burns and cuts off near zero speed. Ground and air are byte-identical with the flag off: `node web/tools/space-parity.mjs --check=Niepotrzebne/space/parity-base.json` hashes every step of 12 scripted drives (ground, boost, ramp, hop, hover, ballistic, assist on and off) and also prints the space checks (drift without gravity, 6.1 m/s² burn, flip 3.9 s, stop 4.5 s, rates damped to 0).
- **Orbital drive**: hold X in orbit (a separate key, so a long Shift + W burn to 88 mph for a time jump does not start it); speed is 0.35 x the distance to the nearest surface (Earth or Moon), so it doubles altitude every few seconds and slows by itself near a body. The Moon is about 30 s away.
- **Return**: O twice (or the tablet `EARTH` button). The landing point is the ground under the Roadster (it moves with the orbit), or a point tapped on the tablet Map (orbit map, which also shows the next lap of the ground track and a `Where I left` button). Within 3 km of the departure point you land exactly where you left; otherwise space calls D's `valley.setLocation(lat, lon, { year })` at the start of the re-entry and waits for it. Re-entry: 5.2 s, altitude 400 to 55 km, plasma sheath on the leading face with a wake, sparks, heat glow, shake and roar, a white flash, then the car is placed (valley spot or `valley.spawn`), `arriveValley()` runs, the camera layers, sky and audio come back. Falling below 120 km in orbit starts a re-entry by itself.
- **Online**: while in space `game.mapId = 'space'`, so `online.roomId()` gives `space_<period>`. With R4 each car sends its orbit (`space.netOffset()` = `orbit.pack()`, 10 numbers: position, orbit normal, frame north, epoch); the receiver propagates that orbit to its own clock and converts the remote pose, velocity and attitude into its own pocket, so players on different orbits see each other where they really are (hidden when more than 40 km away). Someone arriving while a friend is in orbit adopts the friend's orbit and appears 25 m from them.

## 3. API (`game.space`)
```js
createSpace(game) -> {
  state,            // 'ground' | 'armed' | 'launch' | 'orbit' | 'armedReturn' | 'reentry'
  active,           // true from the teleport until the landing
  press(), cancel(),// the tablet button and O (two presses)
  info(),           // { state, active, alt, cruise, label, earth, sunlit, landing: {lat, lon}, landingName, sub, picked }
  setTarget(lat, lon), target,   // landing pick from the tablet map (null = under the car)
  altitude(), offset, look,      // metres above the surface, frame offset, current look (look.js)
  netOffset(), netAdjust(remote),// co-op frame sync (R4)
  preStep(dt), update(fdt),      // main.js hooks (already wired)
}
```
URL: `?nospace` skips the module, `?space` boots straight into orbit (debug), `?spaceroom=valley` keeps the valley room while in space (co-op test without the new RTDB rule).

- **R4 Co-op frame sync (online.js, three lines).** Without it, players who used the drive or flew more than 2.6 km see each other offset.
  ```js
  // send(): after building d
  if (g.space && g.space.active) d.s = g.space.netOffset();
  // Remote.push(d): with the other fields
  this.so = Array.isArray(d.s) ? d.s : null;

  ```
- **R5 Thruster plumes in vacuum (fx.js).** In space the plumes billow like smoke on the ground. Suggest: when `sim.env.space`, no buoyancy, a third of the particle life and twice the expansion, so the gas thins out fast.
- **R6 Chase camera in space (camera.js, optional).** The yaw-only chase camera swings when the car points straight up or down. A full-attitude follow (behind and above in the car frame, slow up-vector blend) would suit space; Earth-up still works fine for normal flying.
- **R7 Landing anywhere (main.js).** After a landing space calls `game.onLanded(location)` if it exists (`location = valley.location`). Outside the valley I set `game.mapId = 'earth'` as a placeholder; please set the real `earth_...` id there.
- **R9 Sun flare in space (info, already shimmed).** Space wraps `gfx.updateSun(dir, colour, k)` and scales `k` by 0.3 while the space root is visible, because the valley-tuned flare looked like a game lens next to the photos. If you prefer it native: give `updateSun` a `this.flareScale` factor (default 1) and I set it instead of wrapping.
- **R8 Ride-along into space (later).** A passenger is dropped when the driver goes up (the driver's room changes). Following the driver into `space_<period>` would need the passenger to switch rooms with them.
- **R10 Story waypoint in orbit (story.js, one condition).** The objective diamond ("1.2 km") is projected onto the valley 6 km under the pocket and floats over the Earth in orbit. In `drawTarget()` first line: `if (!T || this.g.paused || this.dlgOpen || (this.g.space && this.g.space.active)) { ... }`.
