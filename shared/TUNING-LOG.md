# Tuning log

One line per change to `tuning.json`: date, engine, key, old -> new, why.

- 2026-10-15 web: added `wheels.inertia_each` = 1.9 kg m^2 (wheel + tyre + reflected motor/gear inertia per corner, used by the wheel-spin ODE; was implicit).
- 2026-10-15 web: added `thrusters.hover_engage_s` = 0.5 (Space held at least this long enters hover/altitude-hold; shorter presses are hops with a minimum burn of `hop_tap_max_s`, so the parity "0.3 s tap" is a pure hop).
- 2026-10-15 lead: real car dimensions from photogrammetry of side photos: wheelbase 2.65 -> 2.85, length 4.47, height 1.13, tyres 265/35R20 front (r 0.347, w 0.265) and 325/30R21 rear (r 0.364), inertia scaled to the longer car, collider box resized, nozzle positions moved to the real bumper/diffuser positions. Re-run parity.
- 2026-10-15 web: `tyres.mu_lat` 1.32 -> 1.45. With COM 0.43 m over a 1.70 m track the lateral load transfer is about 33 percent at the limit and `load_sensitivity` eats the rest, so 1.32 only gave 1.18 g steady state at 60 mph; 1.45 gives about 1.29 g (SPEC: lateral grip about 1.3 g).
- 2026-10-15 web: `powertrain.brake_force` 32000 -> 23000 N. 32 kN is above the tyre limit (ABS-limited about 1.45 g, 60-0 in 25.5 m); SPEC wants about 32 m on tyres only, which needs about 1.15 g at the tyres (wheel spin-down inertia takes about 3 percent of the pedal force). Brake force is the total pedal force including blended regen (no extra regen while braking).
- 2026-10-15 lead: front nozzles moved behind the flap (x ±0.13, y 0.30, z 2.10 at the gimbal), rear track 1.74 -> 1.69 so the 325 rear tyres sit inside the hips. CONTRACT nozzle rotation signs fixed (rear: -90° about X = down, front: +90° = down).
- 2026-10-16 web: added `damage.wheel_break_impulse` 30000 N s, `damage.nozzle_break_impulse` 9000 N s, `damage.glass_falloff_m` 0.6 (glass impulse = impact impulse x exp(-distance / falloff)), and `damage.crush` (crush force per zone and soft-crush length; crush depth = impact energy / force, cabin force past the soft length, capped at `max_depth`). 30 mph into a wall gives about 0.4 m of front crush, 80 mph about 1.3 m, 200 mph folds the car to the cap.
- 2026-10-16 lead (Jurek playtest: too little steering, brakes too slow, car slides): max_steer 34 -> 38, steer falloff full_until 8 -> 14 / min 0.22 -> 0.36 at 70 -> 90, steer_rate 260 -> 420, mu_long 1.52 -> 1.75, mu_lat 1.45 -> 1.75, peak_slip_angle 7 -> 6, falloff_long 0.82 -> 0.92, falloff_lat 0.86 -> 0.95, load_sensitivity 0.07 -> 0.04, brake_force 23000 -> 42000. Feel over the 32 m spec number. Web also cut the low-speed steer slide TC relief from 0.6 to 0.15 (code); Godot should mirror it.
- 2026-10-16 lead (Jurek: slides badly in the air): added `flight_assist.lateral_grip` 4.5 1/s, the assist asks the allocator to cancel sideways velocity (hover and ballistic, capped at 16 m/s^2); `side_deflect_deg` 15 -> 30 on both pairs so the nozzles have real sideways authority.
